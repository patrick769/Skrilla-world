Notes:
  * Hero images are graded in CSS, never baked in — they are desaturated slightly
    and lifted per image so all six pages sit at the same exposure. If you swap a
    hero photo, adjust the matching `.hero--*` rule in the page's <style> block.
  * Video thumbnails come from the official YouTube stills (i.ytimg.com).
  * IMG_0548.PNG at the project root is the untouched original portrait.
