Drop your own gallery images here (jpg/png), then add a card in index.html.

Copy one of the commented ITEMS entries and point photo.url at the new file, for example:

  ./assets/album.jpg
  ./assets/tour.jpg
  ./assets/merch.jpg

Video cards already use the official YouTube thumbnails from videos.html.
The SKRILLA card uses IMG_0548.PNG at the project root.
